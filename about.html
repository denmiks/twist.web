About Twist

We grew up around these orchards.

Twist delivers consistent mango purée, calamansi juice, and concentrated calamansi iced tea to the restaurants, hotels, and manufacturers that need them week after week.

About Us

Established in 2018, TWIST PH is a small Filipino fruit and beverage company founded by Ricardo F. Galguerra Jr. We create quality products inspired by the natural abundance and distinct flavors of the Philippines. As a small business, we continue to improve our products and operations, and we are looking forward to growing in the future.

Our Story

TWIST PH began with Mango Puree, inspired by the mango season when many ripe and overripe mangoes were left unused or wasted. Seeing an opportunity to make better use of these fruits, Ricardo F. Galguerra Jr. developed a business centered on turning surplus mangoes into a useful and delicious product. The product line later expanded to Pure Calamansi and Iced Tea Concentrated with calamansi.

From orchard to your order

The same four steps, whether you're ordering a 1 kg pack or a larger 5 kg format.

1

Harvest

We source quality fruit and ingredients selected for the taste and consistency our products require.

2

Sort & grade

Every batch is checked for taste, texture, sweetness, and acidity before it is approved for packing.

3

Process & pack

Purée and juice are processed, pasteurized, and packed in practical formats for foodservice and production kitchens.

4

Deliver

Chilled trucks run fixed routes into Metro Manila and nearby provinces, with next-day delivery available for standing orders.

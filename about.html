About Twist

We grew up around these orchards.

Twist connects mango growers in Zambales and calamansi growers in Batangas to the restaurants, hotels, and manufacturers that need them, week after week — not just at harvest time.

Why we started

Twist began with a familiar problem for anyone buying fruit for a commercial kitchen: mango and calamansi are two of the most-used ingredients on a Filipino menu, and two of the hardest to source consistently. Quality swings from delivery to delivery. Volume disappears in the off-season. Purée and juice, when available at all, rarely arrive with any real quality control.

We come from farming families in Zambales and Batangas, and we started Twist to close that gap — building direct relationships with growers, investing in a proper cold chain, and processing fruit ourselves so restaurants and manufacturers get the same fruit, the same way, every time they order.

Iced tea concentrate came next, at the request of a few long-standing accounts who wanted one supplier handling both their fruit program and their beverage base. We brew and bottle it in-house, to the same standard we hold our juice to.

From orchard to your order

The same four steps, whether you're ordering a sack of fresh mango or a pallet of juice pouches.

1

Harvest

We work with growers on picking schedules tuned to ripeness stage, not just the calendar — so fruit is cut when it's actually ready for its intended use.

2

Sort & grade

Every lot is sorted by size, color, and sugar content, and only fruit that clears our standard is approved for packing or processing.

3

Process & pack

Purée and juice are pulped, pasteurized, and packed the same day the fruit arrives at our facility — fresh fruit goes straight into ventilated crates.

4

Deliver

Chilled trucks run fixed routes into Metro Manila and nearby provinces, with next-day delivery available for standing orders.

What we hold ourselves to

We grow our own mangoes and calamansi

Our family orchards in Zambales and Batangas supply every batch — no middlemen, no layers of traders — so we know exactly where each fruit comes from.

Consistency over hype

We'd rather deliver the same reliable fruit fifty weeks a year than chase the best-looking lot for one big order.

Built around your kitchen

Pack sizes, order minimums, and delivery days are set to match how your kitchen or line actually runs.

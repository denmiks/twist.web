FAQs

What products do you offer?

We supply Mango Purée, Pure Calamansi, and Concentrated Iced Tea.

Do you offer small pack sizes?

Yes. Mango Purée and Pure Calamansi are available in 1 kg and 5 kg packs. Concentrated Iced Tea is available in a 1 kg pack.

Can I request a custom supply plan?

Absolutely. We can align volume, delivery schedule, and pack sizes to how your business orders.

Do you serve businesses only?

No. We also sell our products on Lazada, where you can check out and place an order. You may also find the Lazada link in our Contact page.

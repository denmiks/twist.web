What we supply

Five core products, each handled the way foodservice buyers actually need it — fresh, processed, or ready to pour.

FM

Fresh Mango

Hand-picked and sorted by size and sweetness, packed in ventilated crates and delivered within 24 hours of harvest. Ripeness stage set to your order.

MP

Mango Purée
(Pouch)

Pulped and pasteurized the same day fruit arrives, packed in foodservice pouches for shakes, sauces, and desserts. Consistent Brix, batch to batch.

FC

Fresh Calamansi

Small-fruited, thin-skinned calamansi sized for juicing or garnish. Available loose by the sack or pre-bagged to a fixed weight.

CJ

Calamansi Juice
(Bottle/Pouch)

Cold-pressed, unsweetened or lightly sweetened. Bottled or pouched from single-serve up to bulk foodservice volumes.

IT

Iced Tea
Concentrate

Brewed strong and bottled at a fixed dilution ratio, so every batch of iced tea pours the same whether it's a slow Tuesday or a full house.
